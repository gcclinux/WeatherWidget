WeatherWidget Update

WeatherWidget v2.2.0 is here 🌤️

Hello there,

WeatherWidget v2.2.0 is out, with smarter tickers, clearer air quality information and wider platform support.

🚀 New

First release for Windows ARM64.

🏃 5 Multi-Ticker Profiles

Instead of raw atmospheric numbers, the ticker now shows tailored opportunity messages:

  • Everyday Outdoors: general context for daily commutes and stepping out.
  • Runner & Cyclist: are conditions and air quality right for outdoor cardio?
  • Family & School Run: practical checks when heading out with children.
  • Sensitive Airways: alerts for asthma, allergy and pollutant sensitivity.
  • Open Windows: is it a good time to let fresh air in?

🌫️ Simplified Pollution Definitions & Grading

Clear graded context (Good, Fair, Moderate and beyond) replaces raw microgram values, with human-readable descriptions alongside the composite AQI scale. Individual toggles (PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, Ozone, CO) are still available.

🛠️ Bug Fixes

  • Linux Snap autostart fixed (existing installs need to disable and re-enable autostart).
  • Windows software rendering now works without a GPU (AMD64 & ARM64).

🗺️ Road Map — Coming Weeks

  • News & Alerts backend: building the services that will power timely weather news and alerts.
  • Refreshed website: showcasing the latest features and releases.
  • WeatherApp News & Alerts: the app will begin delivering news and alerts straight to you.
